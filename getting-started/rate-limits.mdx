Rate limits depend on your active plan: **Free**, **Starter**, **Build**, **Growth**, or **Enterprise**. Every plan has a global per-minute request limit, and most endpoints also have their own per-minute, daily, or monthly cap. Enterprise plans ship with custom limits configured around your committed usage, endpoint requirements, and deployment needs.

## Notes

- Enterprise ships with custom rate limits on every item above, starting from \$2,000/month billed annually.
- Company Search, Industry Search, and the two Filters endpoints are the only daily-reset limits; every other endpoint resets per minute or per month.
- Company Addition requests are billed monthly and are not included on the Free or Starter plans.

## What counts as a request?

Each request to the News Signals, Company Data, List Generation, Alternative Data (Job Posts, Social Posts, Product Reviews, Website Traffic, Employee Reviews, Headcount Trends), and supporting endpoints (Company Search, Industry Search, Filters, Company Addition) counts toward your plan's rate limit.

<Info>
  Usage for all endpoints is charged in credits. Rate limits control request throughput; credit usage controls billing.
</Info>

## Pricing

Rate limits are separate from pricing. For cost information, see [Pricing](/getting-started/pricing).

## Need higher limits?

If you need higher throughput, custom endpoints, de-duplicated feeds, or committed-volume pricing, contact [sales@akta.pro](mailto:sales@akta.pro) with your expected usage volume and requirements.
