Rate limits depend on your active plan. Every plan has a global per-minute request limit, and most endpoints also have their own daily or monthly cap. Higher-tier plans include higher throughput, while Enterprise plans can be configured with custom limits based on committed usage, endpoint requirements, and deployment needs.

\* Includes the markdown and concise response variants of this endpoint — they share the same limit. † No separate limit is configured for this endpoint beyond the plan's global per-minute limit.

<Info>
  "Not available" means the endpoint is not included in that plan and requests to it return a 403. Where both a per-minute and a per-month limit apply to the same endpoint, both are enforced — whichever is reached first will throttle the request.
</Info>

## What counts as a request?

Each request to the News Signals, Company Data, List Generation, Alternative Data (Job Posts, Social Posts, Product Reviews, Website Traffic, Employee Reviews, Headcount Trends), and supporting endpoints (Company Search, Industry Search, Filter Builder, Company Addition) counts toward your plan's rate limit.

<Info>
  Usage for all endpoints is charged in credits. Rate limits control request throughput; credit usage controls billing.
</Info>

## Pricing

Rate limits are separate from pricing. For cost information, see [Pricing](/getting-started/pricing).

If you need higher throughput, custom endpoints, de-duplicated feeds, or committed-volume pricing, contact [sales@akta.pro](mailto:sales@akta.pro) with your expected usage volume and requirements.
